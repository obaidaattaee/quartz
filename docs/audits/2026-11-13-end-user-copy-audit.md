## 5. Top 10 changes

1. **Finish the legal documents.** Replace every drafting placeholder with verified business information and approved contractual provisions.
2. **Resolve the map’s evidence conflict.** Label illustrations consistently and stop directing buyers to “full case studies” that are not available.
3. **Rewrite the Arabic home page from the intended meaning.** Prioritise “ارتباطاتنا”, “طبقة قدرات”, “وضعيات”, “حزمتك”, “وقت ومواد” and the translated slogans.
4. **Make the contact action accurate.** Change “Book a Call” to “Request a call”, explain the response process, and carry the home page’s response expectation onto `/contact`.
5. **Add direct service links to the home summaries.** Let operations buyers reach automation and CTOs reach QA/security without another navigation search.
6. **Unify form validation.** Show the ten-character requirement beforehand, use human field-specific messages in both languages, and associate and announce errors and success.
7. **Make the people and expertise identifiable.** Add verifiable team/lead information and, where available, sanitised QA/security deliverable examples without inventing clients or results.
8. **Preserve enquiry context.** Retain drafts across language switches and carry the selected service, subservice and offer into the form.
9. **Make illustration disclaimers readable and interactions accessible.** Increase caption contrast, provide keyboard alternatives to building selection, and correct radio-group keyboard behaviour.
10. **Standardise the bilingual terminology and remaining English copy.** Use one Blog label, name RSS explicitly, fix feed origins and metadata, and replace jargon and repeated contrast slogans with concrete descriptions.
